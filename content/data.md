#### Monetary policy shocks for India

Monthly series of monetary policy shocks for India, updated through February 2023.

+ [Download the data (Google Sheets)](https://drive.google.com/open?id=128XNenwpN6wCfEnBaZX9s4MIEr6KnmGsaWdHybpcH4s)
+ [Download the data (CSV)](https://aeimit.weebly.com/uploads/2/5/5/8/25585085/monshocks_india.csv)

The construction of the series is described in the paper [Measuring monetary policy shocks in emerging economies: Evidence from India](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jmcb.13144), with [Aeimit Lakdawala](https://aeimit.weebly.com/) ([working paper version](http://www.igidr.ac.in/pdf/publication/WP-2021-021.pdf)).

**Please cite:** Lakdawala, Aeimit, and Rajeswari Sengupta. "Measuring monetary policy shocks in emerging economies: Evidence from India." *Journal of Money, Credit and Banking*, 2024.

#### Legal restrictions on foreign institutional investors in India

A dataset on the evolution of capital controls on foreign institutional investment in India, described in [Legal restrictions on foreign institutional investors in a large, emerging economy: A comprehensive dataset](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352340919311746) (with Radhika Pandey, Aatmin Shah and Bhargavi Zaveri), *Data in Brief*, 2019.
